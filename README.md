# Kurland

Kurland is an interactive iOS experience designed to help build
self-esteem in children with curly and coily hair.

Winner of Apple's Swift Student Challenge 2024

## About

In Kurland, children explore a magical kingdom while completing
interactive activities centered around self-esteem and hair acceptance.

The experience combines storytelling with camera, voice and
motion-based interactions.

## Technologies

- Swift
- SwiftUI
- AVFoundation
- Speech
- CoreMotion

## Features

- Interactive storytelling experience
- Camera-based activities
- Voice recognition interactions
- Motion-based mini-games
- Original characters and visual experience

## Development

Kurland was designed and developed as an individual project for
Apple's Swift Student Challenge 2024, from concept and prototyping
to implementation.
